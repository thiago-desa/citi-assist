# Skill: Grounded formal draft

Draft a submission for a target OfficialChannel.

## Job

Draft the formal submission. Every factual claim must cite a specific Evidence node or exact source. Do not infer causation from co-occurrence. Include the desired outcome and a relevant regulation only if confirmed. Format for the target OfficialChannel (email body, form fields, or letter text).

## Writing style

Apply `skills/writing-style.md` on every draft and follow-up: sincere appreciation where earned, people separated from the problem, calibrated "How"/"What" questions, objective criteria from the agency's own publications, and a clear next escalation held in reserve. Keep channel-appropriate structure.

## Approval

Present the draft to the citizen. Do not send or submit until they approve the exact text and recipient.
