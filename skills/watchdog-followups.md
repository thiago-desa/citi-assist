# Skill: Watchdog follow-ups

Proactive scan of open cases.

## Cadence (as run by the live bot)

Weekdays around 09:00 America/Vancouver.

## Job

1. Scan open Issues for FollowUps that are due or stalled.
2. Suggest status checks, polite inquiries, or escalations along the mapped path.
3. Bring drafts and decisions that need the citizen.
4. Stay silent if nothing needs attention.

## Rules

- Never invent ticket or reference numbers.
- Never escalate past a step the citizen has not approved when the step requires a new submission.
- Prefer updating the graph after every completed check.
